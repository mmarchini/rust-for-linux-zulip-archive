Miguel Ojeda (Oct 14 2026 at 12:18):

Is the idea that, once we have something minimally working, we can submit to a past subtree branch that apparently needs reviving such as staging/rust-net for initial feedback? And if all is resolved, continue on from there? Pushing early and often?

It is up to the people that want to work on it, and the upstream maintainers.

i.e. we did have topic branches back then so that people could collaborate in a central, neutral place; but it is of course not a requirement to use that at all. I am happy to create branches like that if it somehow helps those interested, but it is just that, a branch we host. In particular, it may not be what upstream maintainers want.

So, in general, I would recommend contacting the upstream maintainers to see how they would like to proceed, whether they would be open to patches in the future in the area, how they would like development to happen and where, etc. I have some general notes about how to proceed here that you may have already seen: https://rust-for-linux.com/contributing#submitting-new-abstractions-and-modules and https://rust-for-linux.com/rust-kernel-policy#how-is-rust-introduced-in-a-subsystem

I hope that helps!
