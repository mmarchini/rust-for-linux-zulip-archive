Andreas Hindborg (Nov 06 2026 at 08:03):

When you provide a mutable reference as a function parameter, the function will be allowed to move out of the thing the reference points to (place?). If you don't want that, you put the reference behind a Pin.

Andreas Hindborg (Nov 06 2026 at 08:03):

Of course your T must be !Unpin for this to work.
