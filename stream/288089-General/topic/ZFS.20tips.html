Gary Guo (Sep 01 2026 at 14:30):

I found that make rustdoc is horribly slow on ZFS (taking like 20 minutes), which is due to grep performing "SEEK_HOLE" call to smartly skip over zero bytes. Unfortunately in ZFS it does a full TXG sync by default. Turning zfs_dmu_offset_next_sync off would fix the performance issue.
