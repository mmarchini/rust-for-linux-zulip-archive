Alexandre Courbot (Sep 25 2026 at 13:03):

7 months is indeed very early to tackle a huge, complex project like the kernel (all the more in Rust!). The prerequisite of a successful contribution is understanding what you are contributing to and how your contribution fits in the project. The kernel is also a bit of an outsider in the OSS world as patches are sent and reviewed the "old way" via email, which doesn't make things as simple as a Github pull request.

The first thing to understand is what you are interested in? Operating systems, Rust, anything else? Any experience in C (which is always useful to understand OS design)? The key to keeping motivation is having an itch to scratch, maintaining reasonable and incremental goals, and taking it easy.

Also nowadays anymore can fire a LLM to find something that appears broken and send a patch that addresses an issue, without really understanding it. Would that be fulfilling? I believe it is a good idea to get started with reasonable contributions that you can do without AI assistance, even if they seem trivial. You are more likely to hit the mark and increase your overall understanding that way.
