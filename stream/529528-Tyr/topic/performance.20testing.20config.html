Deborah Brouwer (Sep 22 2026 at 21:56):

I started from the GFXBench config config.tyr.performance and then made these changes:

  • enable DRM_PANTHOR as a module
  • default CPUFreq governor to performance
  • enable devfreq performance governor
  • disable ftrace
  • disable PM debugging
  • disable NFS and SUNRPC debugging
  • enable CONFIG_SCHED_CLUSTER

Sami Tolvanen (Nov 12 2026 at 20:14):

I re-ran GFXBench on my board with the current tyr-dev and while the Vulkan benchmarks are on par with Panthor, I noticed that GL benchmarks are lagging behind currently. I'm not entirely surprised since nobody has looked into optimizing this branch yet.

I uploaded some fixes and optimizations that I've been working on recently and they seem to fix the GL benchmark issue. Here's a comparison:

Benchmark Panthor tyr-dev sami/tyr/7.4/fixes
vulkan_5_normal_off 28.83 FPS 28.83 FPS 28.83 FPS
vulkan_5_normal 57.60 FPS 57.60 FPS 57.61 FPS
gl_5_normal 54.34 FPS 49.83 FPS 59.53 FPS
gl_manhattan31_off 35.08 FPS 31.85 FPS 39.32 FPS
gl_manhattan31 57.29 FPS 52.30 FPS 60.02 FPS (vsync cap)
gl_trex_off 103.22 FPS 102.01 FPS 108.56 FPS
gl_trex 62.21 FPS 62.20 FPS 62.17 FPS (vsync cap)

Note that the plan is to switch to a different JobQueue implementation once it's upstream, so we'll have to re-test after that.

What comes to power consumption, I don't believe Rock 5B provices a convenient way to measure that. We have power measurements from a different device with an ODPM, but this is on AM hardware/driver, so the numbers are not relevant for upstream Tyr.
