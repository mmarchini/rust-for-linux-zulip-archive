Deborah Brouwer (Sep 22 2026 at 21:56):

I started from the GFXBench config config.tyr.performance and then made these changes:

  • enable DRM_PANTHOR as a module
  • default CPUFreq governor to performance
  • enable devfreq performance governor
  • disable ftrace
  • disable PM debugging
  • disable NFS and SUNRPC debugging
  • enable CONFIG_SCHED_CLUSTER
