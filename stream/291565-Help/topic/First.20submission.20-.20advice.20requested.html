Miguel Ojeda (Nov 18 2026 at 02:23):

should I hold off submitting the WMI abstractions until these are complete?

No, you can submit it whenever you feel it is ready enough, but you will want to mention clearly the dependency; otherwise, it can be confusing.

Regarding documentation and testing: that is part of getting submissions ready for upstream. How much polish it needs depends a bit on the maintainer/subsystem, but docs and testing are the sort of thing that if it is not done initially, then it may never get done :) It also helps reviewers, so it is good to add it before submitting. I would suggest taking a look at what we have elsewhere, to give you an idea of what you could do.

I hope that helps, and welcome!
